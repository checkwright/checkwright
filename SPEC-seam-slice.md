**Stated as rules and not as a roster**, for the parent's own reason: a roster of sites rots

roster still describes the tree.

## What changes

### (1) A dated attestation used as a specimen of the idiom it illustrates is not a stamp

One site in this corpus is a dated attestation sitting **inside a rule, as that rule's own
worked example** of the idiom the rule governs {design-bearing}. Every class the parent's
taxonomy defines would sweep it, and sweeping it would delete the demonstration that makes the
rule readable — leaving a rule about how to write a dated attestation with no dated attestation
in it.

**The test is the date's referent, not its form.** A date naming an event in this project's
history is provenance and is swept. A date inside a specimen — a constructed instance the
surrounding prose is teaching the shape of — denotes nothing that happened, so there is no
private fact to publish and nothing for a consumer to fail to resolve. It is the same reason the
parent's fenced-block exemption exists one tier down: a grammar being *shown* is not a rule being
*stated*.

**The disambiguator, because the two look identical in isolation:** remove the passage and ask
what breaks. If a claim loses its evidence, it was an attestation. If a rule loses its
illustration, it was a specimen. A specimen whose date could be replaced by any other date
without the surrounding prose becoming false is a specimen.

### (2) A live procedural status is swept and is not migrated

One passage closes on an **unresolved, present-tense procedural fact** — that this project's
operator was asked something and had not answered when the work closed {design-bearing}. This is
neither settled history nor mechanism, and the parent's classes do not reach it: it is not a
stamp, not a pointer, not a label, and not a refused alternative's grounds.

**It is swept, and delta 3's move-versus-delete test does not apply to it.** That test routes a
*finished* ruling to deletion and a *live load-bearing* ground to migration, and this is
neither — an open ask is not a ruling at all. The ruling record's own authoring rule takes what
has no other durable home *and is a ruling*; filing an open question there would put a
non-ruling in the register of rulings.

**So the disposition is two-part and the split is what makes it safe.** Whatever engineering
content the passage carries — the substantive reason the question is worth asking — stays,
stated impersonally and with no ask attached. The *status* goes, and if the question is still
open when the sweep runs it routes to the queue or the gap inbox, which are the surfaces that
hold open work. A kit SPEC publishing that an unnamed authority owes an unnamed answer tells a
consumer nothing they can act on and tells them something about this project they were not owed.

### (3) Where an attribution abuts undated numeric evidence, the cut lands between them

Three sites in this corpus put an attribution clause **immediately beside** undated numeric
evidence — a citation count, a pointer count, a run of measurements at a stated precision
{design-bearing}. A paragraph-granular sweep takes both; a clause-granular one takes the right
half.

**The numbers are on the parent's exclusion side and the attribution is on its sweep side, and
the exclusion does not depend on the number carrying a date.** The parent excluded a *dated*
measurement on the ground that its date is its freeze. An **undated** measurement has strictly
less provenance attached and strictly the same evidential job, so nothing in the discriminator
reaches it: it names no authority, no date, no channel and no internal identifier. It stays for
the ordinary reason engineering prose stays.

**Recorded as its own rule rather than left to care**, because the failing shape here is not
misjudgment — it is granularity. A sweep that decides per paragraph gets this class wrong every
time while applying the discriminator correctly, which is why the rule is about where the cut
lands rather than about which side each half falls on.

### (4) A passage's claim to be its own sole home is evidence about the mechanism, never about the stamp

One site asserts, in its own words, that a ruling's home is *here* because this is where a later
reader meets it {design-bearing}. Read as a sole-home claim it looks like delta 3's *moves*
case; read against delta 3's actual test it is not, and the difference is worth a rule because
this shape recurs wherever a SPEC section explains its own existence.

**Delta 3's test applies to the ruling's *subject*, not to the passage's self-description.** If
the subject is finished, the stamp is deleted outright — the ruling record's completion-time
half says so, and it says so precisely to stop finished rulings accumulating. A passage insisting
on its own local relevance is making a claim about **where the mechanism belongs**, which is
true and untouched: the mechanism stays, in that section, stated undated and impersonally. What
the claim cannot do is keep the *authority and the date* attached to it, because the reason a
later reader meets the rule there is a reason about the rule, not about who ruled it or when.

**The one shape that would genuinely be delta 3's moves case is different and is named so the
rule is not over-applied:** a ground still load-bearing for a **live** rule, with no other home,
where deleting it would leave the live rule unsupported. That migrates. A finished ruling in a
section that explains why it is the right section does not.

### (5) The pointer class ships intact for the third consecutive unit, and its answer is owed before the gate

The `CLAUDE.md §The provenance seam` citations are **not** swept by this amendment
{design-bearing}. Two of them fall in this corpus — one in `lifecycle-kit/SPEC.md`, one in
`delegation-kit/SPEC.md` — and `queue-kit/SPEC.md` carries none, which is consistent with the
record's five-kit breakdown re-verified this session as still exact at seventeen.

**The question is answerable for every kit SPEC at once or not at all**, which is what makes
this slice the wrong place to answer it: a ruling taken here would bind five kits nobody has
swept and would be applied by a session that surveyed three. The parent shipped its ten intact,
the first slice shipped its nine, and this one ships its two on the same ground — deliberately,
not by omission, and a reader meeting a swept file with a live `CLAUDE.md` citation in it should
read that as this rule holding.

**When it must be answered is stated, because "open" with no deadline is how it has stayed open
across three units.** The seam gate encodes whichever way it goes, so the answer is owed **before
the slice that lands the gate** and not before any earlier one. It is a derivation rather than a
ruling — the citations resolve in this tree and in no vendoring consumer's — and it is escalated
with this amendment rather than taken.

### (6) The gate is not this slice's, and the enforcement-first objection is deferred rather than dissolved

No gate lands here {design-bearing}. Five kits still owe after this slice, and a seam gate would
red on every one of them from the commit it landed in.

**Saying so is the delta.** Enforcement-first rules that the fix and the gate that catches it
land in one unit, and a by-kit split defers that pairing every time it slices — the parent
recorded this and the deferral does not get cheaper by repetition. What keeps it honest is that
the deferral has a stated end: the gate lands with the last slice, the entry that owns it says
so, and until then every swept kit ships ungated and a re-accreted site would go unnoticed.

**And the accretion is measured rather than feared** — the entry records two new sites landing in
one iteration from cuts that were not editing provenance, which is why the cost of the deferral
is stated on each slice rather than assumed to be flat.

### (7) The rank order is confirmed for this slice's three, and the confirmation is bounded

The parent's rank order — lifecycle, delegation, queue, then five more — was recorded as
holding qualitatively and failing quantitatively {mechanical}. Across this slice's three the
census matches the order monotonically, each file carrying roughly half the previous rank's
sites.

**This neither confirms nor refutes the parent's finding, and the amendment says so** rather
than letting a clean local result read as a general vindication: the parent's quantitative
failure was observed at other ranks, and three ordered files cannot adjudicate a claim about the
ones after them. The order continues to order and continues not to count.

## Producers and consumers

**No new state, event, interface, field or knob.** This delta set adds four decision rules and
removes prose; it mints nothing a component must honour and nothing a machine reads. The
producers and consumers below are therefore the *corpus's*, in the shape the parent's amendment
established for the same work.

**The swept prose.** *Producer* — the build session, applying the recovered discriminator and
the four rules above to the three files as it finds them. *Consumers* — three, all of them
readers of text and none of anything structured, so no field is added and none has a reader to
name: the generated docs mirror, produced by its rostered emit arm and held byte-equal by its
freshness gate, whose transition is the pre-commit hook; every adopter's vendored copy, assembled
from these kit roots at release and read by a human; and a session loading a section at its own
load trigger.

**The engineering content that survives delta 2's split.** *Producer* — the same session,
restating the substantive reason impersonally in the section it already sits in. *Consumer* — a
later reader of that section, at its load trigger. Nothing migrates to the ruling record under
this delta, which is the delta's point.

**The open question delta 2 may surface.** *Producer* — the build session, if the ask is still
open when it reaches the site. *Consumer* — the gap inbox or the queue, whichever the finding's
class routes it to; the ruling record is explicitly **not** a consumer here, and delta 2 states
why.

**This delta set narrows a corpus — the prose a reader can find in three kit SPECs — so the
causal-completeness check's point 5 binds and each reader's RED condition is enumerated rather
than its subject. All three files select the same thirty-gate core set; `delegation-kit/SPEC.md`
additionally selects `check-rule-citation`.**

- **`check-md-refs`** reds on a reference resolving to nothing. Deleting a `TRAJECTORY.md`
  pointer can only remove references, so its verdict is monotone here and it is clearable by
  inspection — except where a delta rewrites a sentence and re-points a surviving reference,
  which the build checks by running it.
- **`check-manifest-count`** reds on **finding** a bare cardinal quantifying a governed
  collection. Delta 4's rewrites and delta 2's impersonal restatements can **introduce** a
  cardinal where none stood, so this reader is not monotone under the rewrite half and is run,
  not inspected.
- **`check-measured-claim`** reds when a `measured:` marker's oracle value disagrees with the
  marker and **fails closed on an unknown key**. Delta 3 exists to keep the sweep off the
  numbers a marker may be bound to; it is the rule that makes this reader safe, not a
  replacement for running it.
- **`check-prose-enum`** holds a **coverage floor** — it reds when two or more members of a
  declared set are hand-listed in one paragraph with a member omitted. Delta 2's split can turn a
  narrated status into a partial enumeration, which is exactly the shape this gate measures, so
  any enumeration the sweep produces is complete or is not a list at all.
- **`check-surface-duplication`** reds on a canonical definition restated outside its owner.
  Deltas 1 through 4 **cite** the parent's recovered discriminator rather than restating it,
  which is what keeps this reader green and is also why this amendment carries no copy of it.
- **`check-rule-citation`**, selected by `delegation-kit/SPEC.md` alone, is named because the
  per-file reader set is not uniform across this corpus and a sweep that clears two files by
  inspection has not thereby cleared the third.
- **`check-unmarked-claim`** and **`check-tracking-claim`** read all three files; delta 2's
  removal of a live procedural status is precisely the shape a tracking claim is made of, so the
  removal is run against them rather than reasoned about.
- **The docs mirror's freshness gate** reds on any byte of drift between source and projection.
  It is monotone in nothing and is cleared only by regenerating.

## Definition of Done

- [ ] **Causal completeness** — the narrowed corpus's readers are enumerated by red condition
      rather than by subject, the non-monotone ones are run rather than inspected, and the
      per-file reader difference is honoured rather than generalized from one file.
- [ ] **Merged with no information lost** — every ground still load-bearing for a live rule
      either stands in its section, undated and impersonal, or has been written into the ruling
      record; no specimen was swept as a stamp, and no measurement was swept as an attribution.
- [ ] **Amendment deleted** — this file removed on merge; none remain at the repo root
      (`ls SPEC-*.md`).
- [ ] **Removals propagated** — every surface pointing at a deleted passage re-checked; the docs
      mirrors regenerated; `check-md-refs` green.
- [ ] **The unit demotes rather than completes** — five kits and the gate still owe, so the entry
      returns to the design-pending section for the next slice's own amendment, and the demotion
      lands at build rather than at the drain.
- [ ] **Gaps filed** — cross-component gaps discovered during the work filed to the gap inbox,
      and any live procedural status delta 2 removes routed to the surface that holds open work.
