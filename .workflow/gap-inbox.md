- 2026-08-28 — OPERATOR PROPOSAL, filed unruled: move the iteration-boundary scratch wipe out of lifecycle-kit/bin/enter-stage.sh and into the lead, fired at iteration start before any stage is dispatched. Raised by the operator 2026-08-28 immediately after the SECOND attestation of boundary-wipe-preserve-lifetime-scope, which happened at this very boundary. It is a FOURTH shape beside the three that entry already names (add the journal basename to the preserve list; give the preserve knob a lifetime vocabulary; move a live journal out of the wiped directory), and it differs from all three in KIND: those change WHAT the wipe preserves, this changes WHO fires it and WHEN. Filed rather than started under scope-gated intake, and deliberately NOT ruled by the lead — the entry is [design-pending] and this is a design fork on it. GROUNDS AGAINST, three, each read off a governed surface by the lead and confirmed by no oracle. (1) THE LEAD IS OPTIONAL. lifecycle-kit/templates/lead.md says outright that an iteration runs correctly with no lead and that each stage is then an ordinary skill invocation. Move the wipe there and a lead-less iteration never wipes .tmp at all — the regression lands precisely on the path that has no supervisor to notice it, and it is silent, because .tmp is gitignored and no gate reads it. (2) THE LEAD WRITES NO LIFECYCLE STATE. The same template makes that its load-bearing invariant: every state write originates in a stage session through enter-stage.sh, and under the stage posture of LIFECYCLE_KIT_SESSION_BOUNDARY a lead-side write is exactly the self-reported skip check-stage-evidence exists to catch. The boundary reset is mechanized motion hung on that entry point; relocating it to the lead moves it out of reach of the stage-entry gates entirely, so the wipe stops being something any gate can see happen. (3) IT PATCHES TIMING, NOT LIFETIME. Firing the wipe before the lead writes its own journal saves that one artifact by ordering alone. Every other session-lifetime artifact is untouched by the change: a consult or teammate session live across the boundary, and — sharper — a <key>.run launch-liveness record whose producer is still alive, which CLAUDE.md already routes into .tmp and which guard-kit rule 14 reads in order to block index-writing git commands. Deleting one of those retracts a true statement about a live process, which is the harm the record rule names in the same breath as forbidding it. The entry frames the defect as WHICH LIFETIMES the preserve list must cover; an ordering change does not answer that question for any member but one. GROUNDS FOR, stated so the refusal is costed rather than waved off: the lead is the one actor that knows an iteration is opening AND holds the identity of the sessions it dispatched, so it is the only party positioned to preserve by liveness rather than by name — which is the vocabulary shape 2 wants. That is a real argument, and it is why this is filed rather than refused outright. Its limit is that the lead-privileged knowledge reaches only sessions the lead itself dispatched, and the attested victim in BOTH attestations is the lead journal, which no dispatch created. COST OF LEAVING IT UNFILED: the shape gets re-proposed at full cost every boundary the recurrence fires, because the entry records neither it nor the three grounds above. DISPOSITION OWED: fold this into boundary-wipe-preserve-lifetime-scope as a recorded-and-refused fourth shape carrying these grounds, or promote it if the drain judges the grounds wrong. Not a new entry — it is a fork on an existing [design-pending] one, and filing it as a sibling would split the design.
