# SPEC amendment: rule-body

Queue entry: `doctrine-rule-body-length-unruled`. Joins unit set `resident-tier-restatement`
under **operator direction, 2026-09-13, lead-relayed**, with the entry's own bound: compressing
rule 11's hotfix valve may not widen or narrow what a session may start — that is operator-class,
never wording. This amendment holds the bound by not rewording the valve at all.

## The question, restated as the entry leaves it

A close brevity pass found two doctrine rule bodies — rule 2 *Enforcement-first* and rule 11
*Scope-gated intake* — longest in the deliverable, neither narration, a count, nor a seam
violation, and could do nothing with them: its licence is compression, and compressing a clause
that narrows a valve re-rules the valve. The entry names the two dispositions: a rule about what a
doctrine body owes its digest, or an explicit acceptance of the length said once. **This amendment
takes both, because they are one answer**: the rule says what a body owes by clause *class*, grades
no length, and so the acceptance of a long valve is a consequence stated once rather than an
exception carved out.

A length target is refused. The entry's candidate signal — a body several times its digest — is
true of every rule in the file, since a digest is one line by construction, so it discriminates
nothing and would be the noisy check the Enforcement-first carve-out keeps out of the battery.

## What changes

### (1) What a rule body owes beyond its digest, ruled by clause class {design-bearing}

`doctrine-kit/SPEC.md` §The doctrine deliverable gains the rule. Its text, **Not yet applied**:

> **What a rule body owes beyond its digest.** A rule's body carries its *statement* — the
> convention, its scope, and every valve with each of its bounds; the *grounds* of a statement
> clause where no other surface owns them; *why it holds under agent work*; and *where it is
> enforced*, with that enforcement's honest limit — and length grades none of them. What is never
> owed, a compression pass removes on sight: a clause a sibling clause
> of the same body already states; a mechanism's operation or defence that a kit SPEC owns, which
> the *Enforced by* pointer already reaches (the Content-tiering rule applied to the doctrine
> itself); and narration of the sessions that attested the rule, whose home is version control. A
> long body whose every clause is owed is correct at its length.
>
> A valve is where that consequence has to be said rather than inferred. A clause bounding what a
> session may start or do is statement, and a compression pass does not reword it even where
> shorter wording looks equivalent: a valve's wording is what a session reads to decide whether it
> is permitted, so changing it re-rules the valve, which enters as a scoped unit — the Scope-gated
> intake rule's own self-exemption clause. No gate grades any of this; whether a clause is owed is
> a judgment about its class, the false-positive carve-out's case, and its cadence is the
> close-stage brevity pass's walk of the doctrine file whenever it grows
> (context-kit/SPEC.md §The close-stage brevity pass).

The owed/never-owed split is the existing doctrine read at the body's grain, not a new
obligation: the file header already says the mechanism lives in the cited kit SPEC and is never
restated, and the Content-tiering rule already forbids a parallel copy. What was unruled is the
residue the entry hit — a clause that is none of the three never-owed classes but is *long* — and
the rule's answer to it is that length is not a class.

### (2) The doctrine file's header points a compressing reader at the rule {mechanical}

The reader who needs delta 1 is whoever is about to shorten a rule body — a close brevity pass
walking the file, or a unit editing a rule. Both open `DOCTRINE.md`; neither opens the doctrine-kit
SPEC unprompted. So the header's existing body-shape sentence (DOCTRINE.md lines 5-8, "Each rule
lands as its statement, why it holds under coding-agent work, and a pointer to the mechanism …")
gains one clause, **Not yet applied**:

> Length is not graded: a clause stays for its class, never goes for its size
> ([SPEC.md](SPEC.md) §The doctrine deliverable).

One line on a load-triggered file, paid for by delta 3's cut.

### (3) Rule 2 loses its three never-owed clauses {design-bearing}

Delta 1 applied to rule 2, clause by clause. Three clauses are a sibling clause restated or a
framing phrase, and go; every other clause is owed and stays byte-identical. **Not yet applied.**

- **"The caveat that keeps this from reading gate-averse:"** — a framing phrase announcing the
  next clause. Replaced by `But`.
- **"— the budget argument never keeps a needed gate out; it keeps removable surfaces from being
  kept because a gate could watch them"** — restates the two clauses before it: *the mechanism is
  bought against a budget … the strongest form is structural* and *no duplication with no gate
  outranks duplication with a gate*, joined to the clause it trails, *the gate is … owed*.
- **"; a duty with no cadence rots exactly like the gap that rule catches"** — restates *a stated
  manual duty carries a named cadence, or it is a duty no session performs*, four lines up.

And the sentence carrying the last cut is re-joined without it: *"the roster plus its review step
are the capture mechanism this carve-out owes, self-applying like the Gap-disposition rule's costed
deferral rather than a gate"* becomes *"the roster with its review step is this carve-out's capture
mechanism rather than a gate"* — the Gap-disposition comparison is itself the restated cadence
clause in a second form.

**What stays, and why each is owed**, recorded because the kept clauses are the ones a later pass
reaches for next:

- The **event-keyed due-ness** clause with its parenthesized events — statement: it is what makes
  the cadence a named one, and the roster file's `due:` field has no other contract for its values.
- **"The roster is hand-curated, not derived — which classes escape a clean check is a judgment no
  tool enumerates, so Derivation-first's ladder lands on state-once-at-the-owner"** — grounds no
  other surface owns. `.workflow/audit-roster.txt`'s `# contract:` header points *at* this rule,
  so the rule is the roster's owner, and a relocation would have no destination (see the gap filed
  below).
- The false-positive carve-out sentence — statement, and cited by name from rules 3, 10, 11, 12 and
  the doctrine-kit SPEC.

The proposed statement paragraph, whole:

> 2. **Enforcement-first.** On any fix or redundancy finding, name the defect
>    class *and* the mechanism that catches it, and land both in one unit; a green
>    instance fix is the stop signal to ask what check should have caught it. When
>    the gate cannot land in the same unit, the instance fix rides the gate's unit

>    hand-curated, not derived — which classes escape a clean check is a judgment

Twenty-three lines against twenty-seven; *Under agent work*, *Enforced by* and the `*Digest:*`

### (4) Rule 11 is audited under delta 1 and keeps every clause {design-bearing}

Delta 1 applied to rule 11 finds nothing never-owed, so **its text does not change** — which is the
disposition the operator's bound on this unit wanted, reached on the rule's own terms rather than
by exemption. The audit, clause by clause:

- *Delivery work enters only through the scope gate*, the file-by-default sentence and its

- *Gap disposition's intake-side sibling* — the scope clause that divides this rule from rule 10;

- *Licenses no self-exemption* — statement, and it is the clause delta 1's valve paragraph cites.
- The valve: *a ruling rather than a judgment call*, the *impacting failure* definition, the three

- *The done section is the closing iteration's ledger … can red the next boundary entry* — grounds
  for bound three with no other owner: lifecycle-kit/SPEC.md §Deviation transitions refuses a

## Producers and consumers

- **The body-owes rule** (delta 1).
  - *Producer:* a session authoring or compressing a doctrine rule body. No trigger, field or
    config is new.
  - *Consumer:* that session, reaching the rule through the doctrine header (delta 2); and the
    close-stage brevity pass, whose walk of a grown doctrine file is the cadence the rule names.
  - *Honest limit:* no gate reads it. A pass that never opens `DOCTRINE.md` never meets delta 2's
    pointer — and needs none, because only a pass or unit editing the file can breach the rule.
- **The header pointer** (delta 2). *Producer:* this unit's build. *Consumer:* the reader above.
  Its citation resolves under `check-spec-pointer` and `check-md-refs`, the two readers a new
  `§`-bearing link puts at risk.
- **Red conditions under the edits** (causal-completeness point 5). Delta 3 narrows rule 2's text;
  no delta narrows a gate corpus. The readers of `DOCTRINE.md`, enumerated by red condition:
  - `check-doctrine-registration` reds on a digest bullet/trailer mismatch (B, C, F), an
    untrailered or doubly-trailered rule (D, E), a missing link (A). No trailer, rule name or rule
    count moves; F compares the unchanged `*Digest:*` value.
  - `--emit stage-rules` reads craft-register `*Stages:*` trailers only; rules 2 and 11 are
    methodology rules. Not reached.
  - `check-manifest-temporal` reds on a `formerly…`-class marker; the added text carries none.
  - `check-md-refs` / `check-spec-pointer` red on an unresolvable link or `§`; delta 2 adds one of
    each, and §The doctrine deliverable exists.
  - `check-manifest-count` reds on a bare cardinal in manifest prose. Delta 1's text is written
    without one and the proposed rule 2 text carries no new number; the build runs the gate rather

  - `check-docs-mirror-fresh` reds on a stale `docs/doctrine-kit/` mirror — regenerated in the

  - Every surface citing a rule by *number*: no rule is added, removed or renumbered, so each
