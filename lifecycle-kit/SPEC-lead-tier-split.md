# SPEC amendment: lead-tier-split

**`lifecycle-kit/templates/lead.md` §Economics' *Split the lead where the tail dominates*
bullet is amended to state the axis its cost argument does not measure.** The template ships
to every consumer vendoring lifecycle-kit, and the claim it ships has been measured against
this repo's own leads for twelve consecutive iterations without the template recording that
the measurement exists.

The unit joins this iteration under the operator's 2026-09-05 ruling, relayed through the
lead session, against a scope recommendation to hold. That composition is closed and is not
reopened here.

**What is being amended is a *premise*, so the amendment says precisely which clause fails
and which holds.** The queue entry's own two-axis reading is the input: on **cost** the
premise holds; on **character** it fails every firing. This amendment converts that reading
into template prose, and refuses the shape that would have been easier — declaring the bullet
wrong — because the cost half is measurably right and deleting it would lose the argument that
makes the split posture worth having at all.

**The provenance seam is clean today and this amendment must leave it clean.** Neither
`lifecycle-kit/templates/lead.md` nor the local binding carries an iteration count, a cost
figure or a date — verified across both files this session; the twelve escalation counts and
the six-point supervision series live only on the queue entry, which is where CLAUDE.md
§The provenance seam puts them. **No measurement enters the template**, and the amendment
states that as a constraint rather than leaving it to the build's judgment, because the
natural way to justify an amendment in prose is to cite the evidence for it.

## What changes

### (1) The bullet gains a character limb, and the cost limb is unchanged

The bullet's failure is not that its claim is false but that its **enumeration is
incomplete**, and naming that precisely is what makes the fix small {design-bearing}.

*Most of a lead's turns are tail* is a distributional claim and it holds. The clause that
fails is the next one — "concentrates judgment-tier spend where it pays: **scope itself, plus
one oracle turn per forwarded intent question**" — which enumerates two judgment-bearing
classes of turn and omits a third: **the turns in which the lead rules a stage's escalation
itself, alone, by reading a governed surface**. Those are neither scope nor a forwarded
question. Under the split posture they do not become cheaper; they become **relays**, because
a routing-tier lead forwards what it cannot rule.

**The amended bullet therefore reads in two limbs under the same lead-in.** The cost limb
keeps its text. The character limb states three things and no more:

- a lead's turn set contains rulings it can make alone off a governed surface, and the
  enumeration above does not cover them;
- the split posture's saving is bought by converting those into relays, which is a change in
  **what the lead does**, not only in what it costs — the relayed question reaches the
  operator or the oracle instead of being answered where the governed surface already answers
  it;
- so the split is a **trade** rather than a pure saving, and a consumer choosing a posture
  should measure the trade rather than assume it away.

**Two shapes are refused, each on its own ground.**

- **Qualifying the premise without stating the axis** — adding "on cost" and stopping — is
  refused because it leaves the next reader exactly where the last twelve were: knowing the
  claim is narrower than it looks and having no instrument. A qualification that does not name
  what it excludes is a hedge.
- **Replacing the cost argument with the character finding** is refused because the cost
  argument is the split posture's whole justification and it measures true. An amendment that
  deleted it would leave the template recommending a posture it no longer argues for.

### (2) The counting method promotes into the template; the threshold and every measurement stay the consumer's

The instrument that found this belongs to every consumer; the numbers it produced belong to
this one {design-bearing}.

**The method is generic mechanism**: *read an iteration's escalations and count how many the
lead ruled alone by reading a governed surface — near zero, the tail premise covers the whole
turn set; consistently several, the character limb is live for that consumer's posture*. It
names no model, no tier, no repository and no date. Under doctrine-kit's
**widest-true-tier placement** it is true for every reader of the bullet, so the template is
where it belongs.

**Three things stay out of the template**, and the amendment enumerates them because each is
the kind of thing a session justifying an edit reaches for:

- the **threshold** at which a consumer acts on the count (this repo's is a third such
  iteration) — a calibration, not a mechanism;
- every **measured series** — the escalation counts and the supervision-over-priced-spend
  figures;
- every **dated stamp** naming who ruled the join and when.

All three keep their current home: the local binding for the threshold, the queue entry for
the series and the stamps. This is the same seam the file already holds, so the amendment
preserves a property rather than establishing one.

**Why the method moves up rather than being cited from below.** The binding currently owns it
and says so — "the discriminator stays here, because setting it is what this binding
contributed". That sentence was true when the method was one repository's idea and stops being
true the moment the template states the finding the method produced: a template that says *the
enumeration is incomplete* and does not say *here is how you would know for your own posture*
ships half an instrument. Delta 3 is that sentence's correction, landed in the same commit so
the binding never misdescribes itself.

### (3) The binding shrinks to a citation plus its own threshold

`.claude/commands/lead.md`'s discriminator paragraph is rewritten to **cite** the template's
method and state only what remains local {mechanical}: the threshold, and the pointer to the
queue entry that holds every reading. Its closing rule — *every reading is the entry's, not
this file's; this file carries no datum* — is unchanged and is what keeps the seam clean.

**The rewrite must shrink rather than grow**, and the reason is a live gate rather than
style: `check-shim-restatement` compares every binding shim against the corpus of each kit's
`templates/**/*.md` on a normalized nine-word n-gram floor, and this shim is clean today only
because it quotes the bullet's seven-word title and nothing more. A rewritten paragraph that
paraphrases the promoted method at length is the shape that trips it.

### (4) The bullet's lead-in is deliberately unchanged, and that is a design decision

*Split the lead where the tail dominates* stays exactly as spelled {design-bearing}. Two
surfaces cite it **verbatim by title** — the local binding twice, and
`.claude/commands/economics.md` where it names what a `supervision` cost row is meant to
validate — and nothing gates a citation to a bullet name, so a retitling would stale both
silently. The title also stays true under the amendment: the bullet still recommends the
split; it now states what the split trades.

**Recorded as a decision rather than left as an omission**, because a reader comparing the
amended bullet with its two limbs will reasonably ask why the lead-in was not re-worded to
match, and the answer is that an ungated citation is a reason to hold a name still.

### (5) The reader-facing page gains the caveat, and the meters do not

`docs/orchestration.md` recommends the split posture to an external reader on the cost half
alone {mechanical}. It gains one sentence naming the trade, pointing at the template rather
than restating the limb, so the page's tier is unchanged.

**drift-kit's meters are checked and not edited**, and the amendment says so because they are
the first place a reader looks. `drift-kit/SPEC.md`'s reserved `supervision` value and
`templates/economics.md` carry generic mechanism only — they exist to *measure* whatever
posture a consumer runs and assert nothing about which posture is right. The amendment changes
what the measurement is read against, not what is measured, so neither surface owes an edit.

## Producers and consumers

**No new state, event or interface is introduced.** This unit edits prose on three governed
surfaces. The causal-completeness points are answered about the one thing it does introduce:
a **method** stated in a template.

**The counting method's producer and consumers.** *Producer* — the amended bullet in
`lifecycle-kit/templates/lead.md` §Economics, which the lead skill loads at its own trigger;
its "enabling configuration" is the template's installation, which
`--install-lifecycle` performs for every consumer and which needs no knob. *Consumers* —
(a) a lead session choosing or reviewing a posture, at the transition where it reads
§Economics; (b) `.claude/commands/lead.md`, which cites the method and supplies the local
threshold, read at the same transition; (c) a close session recording an iteration's count
onto the governed entry that holds the series. Each reads the method as prose; nothing
machine-reads it, which is why delta 2's placement rule is stated rather than gated.

**Every field has a named reader.** The method introduces one datum per iteration — the count
of escalations a lead ruled alone off a governed surface. Its reader is the queue entry that
holds the series, at the transition where close records the iteration's reading; the template
neither stores it nor names a store, which is what keeps the datum consumer-side.

**This delta set narrows no corpus**, so point 5 does not bind. The readers whose verdicts
decide whether the edit landed cleanly are named instead, with their red conditions:

- **`check-shim-restatement`** reds when a binding shim shares a normalized nine-word n-gram
  with any kit's `templates/**/*.md`. Delta 3's shrink is what keeps it green, and it is the
  one reader this unit can trip by doing its job carelessly.
- **`check-skill-binding`** reds on an unbound slot or an orphan binding. `lead.md` is a
  boundary skill coupled by name at `tier=precommit`, so any edit re-fires it; neither delta
  touches the `ruling-config` or `escalation-guard` slots, so it is clearable by inspection
  and is run anyway.
- **canon-kit's manifest and prose gates do not reach this file.** Measured: the prose-surface
  finder admits a candidate only if it bears no binding slot, and `lead.md` bears two, so it
  self-excludes from the manifest set. Stated because the natural assumption is the opposite
  and a build session would otherwise hunt for a red that cannot fire.
- **The footprint and value-rollup freshness gates** red on any line-count change to a
  measured surface; `templates/` is in the load-triggered tier, so both stale and both are
  regenerated in the same commit.
