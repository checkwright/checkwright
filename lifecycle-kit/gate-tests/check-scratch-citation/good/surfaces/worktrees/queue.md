# Queue — the three retrieval-pointer forms, none of them tagged

- **single-line-colon** — the attested `prose-profile` form, colon and path on one physical line.
  Full finding and its two-command witness: `.workflow/survey-record.md`

- **markdown-link** — the pointer as a link target rather than a trailing token.
  The rest of the census lives in [the survey record](.workflow/survey-record.md) and is not
  repeated here.

- **wrapped-colon** — the live shape: the colon ends one physical line and the path opens the next,
  so a scanner reading physical lines in isolation clears it with no tag needed.
  The census and its two-command witness:
  `.workflow/survey-record.md`. Everything else in this entry is prose.
