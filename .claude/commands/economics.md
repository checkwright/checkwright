## Bindings

**posture** — **mixed, and the roster lives in one place: the lead command's
ruling-config binding** (`.claude/commands/lead.md` → lifecycle-kit/templates/lead.md
§Economics), which is authoritative here rather than restated. Read it at run time
and judge what it actually says: the tiering rulings churn, and a report that judges
a posture off a stale statement of what the posture *is* reaches a wrong verdict on
correct numbers. As of this writing that binding puts the lead and the stages on
Opus by the stage agent's `model: opus` default, with `align` and `validate` on
`model: sonnet` overrides and `build` tiered **per batch** off its deltas'
work-class labels — so a build row may be either class, and which one is a fact
about that iteration's batches rather than about the stage.

The verdict is therefore **per row, against the tier that row actually rode**: did a
row on the judgment tier earn it, and does a row on the cheaper tier attest the
saving? Two rows need naming rather than folding into that sweep. **`supervision`
is the lead's own burn**, and what the split posture assumes those turns to be is
lifecycle-kit/templates/lead.md §Economics's to say (*Split the lead where the tail
dominates*). So a supervision row priced like a judgment stage is that assumption
failing rather than a stage cost, and the report says so when the numbers show it.
And a **`+fanout`** row is a subtree's aggregate
with no dispatch-type dimension, so it never reports the cost of forking.
